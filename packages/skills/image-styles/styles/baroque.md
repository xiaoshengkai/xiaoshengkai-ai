# 巴洛克风格 (baroque)
## 视觉特点
强烈明暗对比，戏剧化构图，华丽装饰，宗教史诗感，力量动势庄严
## 英文 Prompt 模板
Baroque style, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, theatrical composition, ornate and lavish decorations, religious and epic atmosphere, powerful and dynamic movement, Caravaggio and Rubens inspired, rich golden tones
## 适用场景
史诗主题、宗教题材、华丽氛围
