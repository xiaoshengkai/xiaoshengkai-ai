# 赛博东方风 (cyber-oriental)
## 视觉特点
霓虹科技+东方建筑，汉字灯牌龙纹，飞檐，未来国风都市美学
## 英文 Prompt 模板
Cyber-oriental style, neon lights and holographic signs mixed with traditional Chinese architecture, Chinese character signs and dragon motifs, flying eaves and pagodas in a futuristic city, fusion of ancient and cyberpunk aesthetics, rain-slicked streets with red lanterns
## 适用场景
未来东方、国风科幻、概念设计
