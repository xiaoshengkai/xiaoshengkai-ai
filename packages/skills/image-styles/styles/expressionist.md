# 表现主义 (expressionist)
## 视觉特点
夸张色彩，变形线条，强烈情绪，压迫焦虑孤独，爆发感
## 英文 Prompt 模板
Expressionist painting style, exaggerated and distorted colors, twisted and angular lines, intense emotional expression, feelings of anxiety and isolation, raw and powerful visual impact, Edvard Munch inspired
## 适用场景
情绪表达、心理主题、强烈氛围
