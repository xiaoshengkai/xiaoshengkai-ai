# 宫崎骏风格 (ghibli)

## 视觉特点
细腻手绘线条，柔和暖色调(浅黄淡绿柔粉)，治愈系自然场景，森林田野云朵，童话氛围，人文温情

## 英文 Prompt 模板
Studio Ghibli animation style, soft hand-drawn lines, warm pastel color palette (soft yellow, pale green, gentle pink), healing natural scenery with forests fields and fluffy clouds, cozy and nostalgic atmosphere, gentle lighting, storybook charm

## 适用场景
治愈内容、自然主题、童话故事
