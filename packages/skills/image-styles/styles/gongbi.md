# 工笔国风 (gongbi)

## 视觉特点
线条细腻工整，色彩精致艳丽，仕女花鸟，宫灯屏风，华美典雅，古典气质

## 英文 Prompt 模板
Fine brushwork Chinese painting style (gongbi), delicate and precise lines, vibrant and rich colors, elegant court ladies, flowers and birds, ornate palace lanterns and screens, luxurious classical Chinese aesthetic, intricate details

## 适用场景
古典美学、人物画像、花鸟主题
