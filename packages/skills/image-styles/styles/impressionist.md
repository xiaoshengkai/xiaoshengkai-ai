# 印象派 (impressionist)
## 视觉特点
松散笔触，自然光影，瞬间色彩感，弱化轮廓，空气感，户外氛围
## 英文 Prompt 模板
Impressionist painting style, loose and visible brushstrokes, natural light and shadow effects, capturing fleeting moments of color, soft edges and atmospheric perspective, plein air outdoor feeling, Monet and Renoir inspired
## 适用场景
自然风景、光影氛围、户外主题
