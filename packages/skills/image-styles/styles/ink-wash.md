# 水墨国风 (ink-wash)

## 视觉特点
淡墨晕染，宣纸纹理，留白构图，山水意境，气韵生动，东方美学

## 英文 Prompt 模板
Ink wash painting style, light ink bleeding on rice paper texture, minimalist composition with poetic empty space, misty mountains and flowing water, traditional Chinese aesthetic, spiritual and ethereal atmosphere, black and gray tones with subtle gradients

## 适用场景
中国传统、东方美学、诗意表达
