# 韩系清新风 (korean-fresh)

## 视觉特点
明亮自然光，柔和肤色，低饱和穿搭，干净背景，清爽温柔，生活感强

## 英文 Prompt 模板
Korean fresh and clean aesthetic, bright natural daylight, soft skin tones, muted pastel fashion, clean minimal backgrounds, fresh and gentle atmosphere, lifestyle photography style, youthful and approachable

## 适用场景
时尚穿搭、生活记录、清新氛围
