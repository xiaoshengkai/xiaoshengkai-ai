# 机械朋克 (machine-punk)
## 视觉特点
钢铁材质，巨大装置，齿轮引擎，工业力量感，硬核科技幻想
## 英文 Prompt 模板
Machine punk style, massive steel structures and industrial machinery, giant gears and engines, raw industrial power, heavy metal textures, factory-like atmosphere, hardcore mechanical fantasy, dark and gritty tones
## 适用场景
工业题材、重型机械、硬核科幻
