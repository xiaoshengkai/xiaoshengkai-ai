# 新中式 (modern-chinese)

## 视觉特点
传统元素+现代设计语言，简洁高级，水墨窗棂屏风山形，金色线条，留白克制

## 英文 Prompt 模板
Modern Chinese style, traditional Chinese elements combined with minimalist contemporary design, ink wash accents, window lattice patterns, mountain silhouettes, gold line accents, clean and sophisticated composition, elegant restraint

## 适用场景
品牌设计、高端空间、现代东方
