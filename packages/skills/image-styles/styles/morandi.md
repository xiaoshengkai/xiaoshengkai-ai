# 莫兰迪风格 (morandi)

## 视觉特点
低饱和灰调色系(灰粉灰蓝浅褐)，静谧柔和，文艺慵懒，色彩和谐无冲突

## 英文 Prompt 模板
Morandi color palette, muted and desaturated tones (dusty pink, gray blue, light brown), soft and quiet atmosphere, harmonious color composition with no harsh contrasts, gentle and literary aesthetic, serene and slightly melancholic mood

## 适用场景
文艺内容、生活方式、静谧氛围
