厚重油彩笔触，漩涡状纹理，高饱和色彩，强烈情绪，自然生命力

Post-impressionist thick oil painting style, heavy impasto brushstrokes, swirling textures, highly saturated colors, intense emotional expression, vibrant natural vitality, Van Gogh inspired, bold and expressive
## 适用场景
