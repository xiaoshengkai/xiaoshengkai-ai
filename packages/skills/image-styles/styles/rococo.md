# 洛可可风格 (rococo)
## 视觉特点
轻盈柔美，粉色奶油色金色，玫瑰花纹，浪漫华丽甜美，贵族沙龙气质
## 英文 Prompt 模板
Rococo style, light and delicate pastel colors (pink, cream, gold), ornate rose patterns and scrollwork, romantic and sweet atmosphere, aristocratic salon elegance, playful and charming, Fragonard inspired
## 适用场景
浪漫主题、甜美氛围、贵族题材
