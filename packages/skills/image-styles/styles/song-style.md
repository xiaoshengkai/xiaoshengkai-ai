# 宋代美学 (song-style)

## 视觉特点
淡雅色彩，留白构图，花鸟山水，器物雅趣，含蓄气韵，克制文人气息

## 英文 Prompt 模板
Song Dynasty aesthetic, muted and refined colors, generous negative space, delicate flowers birds and landscapes, scholarly objects and tea ware, restrained elegance, literati spirit, quiet and contemplative atmosphere

## 适用场景
文人雅集、茶道器物、淡雅生活
