# 蒸汽朋克 (steampunk)
## 视觉特点
维多利亚时代工业美学，齿轮管道蒸汽，黄铜怀表飞行器，复古机械幻想
## 英文 Prompt 模板
Steampunk style, Victorian era industrial aesthetic, brass gears and copper pipes, steam-powered machinery, clockwork mechanisms, pocket watches and airships, retro-futuristic fantasy, warm metallic tones, intricate mechanical details
## 适用场景
复古科幻、机械幻想、维多利亚题材
