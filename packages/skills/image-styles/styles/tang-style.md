# 唐风美学 (tang-style)

色彩华丽浓郁，人物丰润大气，服饰纹样精美，盛唐气象，雍容华贵，东方繁盛

## 英文 Prompt 模板
Tang Dynasty aesthetic, rich and luxurious colors, full-bodied elegant figures, exquisite costume patterns and brocade, golden age of Chinese civilization, opulent and magnificent atmosphere, grand imperial style

## 适用场景
历史题材、文化展示、盛世主题
